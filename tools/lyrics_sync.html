A quick guide to accurate timing

1. Tap each word. Play the song and press W or tap Mark word at the beginning of every sung word. The highlighted card tells you which word is next.

2. Finish the line. Press E when the final word finishes, including its held note. The next line is selected. Wait through any instrumental, then mark its first word.

3. Handle an immediate transition. If the next line begins with no gap, press N or Next line now. That ends this line and marks the next line’s first word together.

4. Review and correct. Space plays or pauses. ← / → seek three seconds. Backspace undoes a timing action. Slow playback helps with quick passages. Select any word to retime it, or Retake line to record that line again.

5. Export. Every word and line end needs a timestamp before downloading. Upload the finished .elrc to the matching song in the Content Manager.

Timing drafts save in this browser when storage is available. The audio file is not saved; reselect the same recording to continue. Keep the page open during timing.
